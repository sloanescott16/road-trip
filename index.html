Hot springs near White Sands

There are no hot springs in or right by White Sands. The nearest public soaking is Truth or Consequences (T or C), NM, about 1 hr 45 min from the park via Las Cruces and I-25. All spots are private bathhouses that sell soaks to walk-ins, clustered within a few blocks downtown.

1Riverbend Hot Springs

100 Austin St, Truth or Consequences, NM 87901

Best for actual pools: outdoor shared pools on the Rio Grande, 95 to 108 F, plus private pools. Open to the public.

Hours
8am to 11pm, last soak 10pm
Price
Shared pool pass about $40 Mon to Thu, $50 Fri to Sun for up to 2, plus $15 per extra person, 4 max.
Ages
No young children (adult-focused, check age policy).
Tip
Book ahead on weekends.
